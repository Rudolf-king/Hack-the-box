## 💡 Lessons Learned

For each lab, I try to focus on more than simply completing the challenge.

I ask questions such as:

* How was the vulnerability discovered?
* Why was the system vulnerable?
* What information revealed the possible attack path?
* Why did the attack technique work?
* What could have prevented the vulnerability?
* What can I apply from this lab to future security assessments?

This approach helps me develop a deeper understanding of cybersecurity rather than simply memorizing solutions.
